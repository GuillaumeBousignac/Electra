# Electra

**Catch lightning. Wire your world. Build the factory yourself.**

Electra is a vanilla+ tech mod about **real, physical electricity**. Copper cables run through your base, storms charge your generators, and every machine works right in front of you — nothing disappears into an abstract network.

Electra doesn't give you a perfect factory. **It gives you the tools to build yours.**

---

## Why Electra?

- **Electricity you can touch.** Bare copper wire is dangerous — walk into a live cable and you'll feel it. Insulate it with wool to stay safe.
- **Power from the sky.** Your first energy source is the storm itself.
- **Everything stays visible.** Chests stay chests, cables exist in the world, factories are something you can walk through.
- **Complexity is optional.** Power a single furnace in five minutes, or build a whole industrial network. Your call.
- **Modpack-friendly.** Uses standard **Forge Energy (FE)** and works alongside Mekanism, Create, Thermal and any other FE mod.

---

## Lightning Collector

Place a **lightning rod** on top, wait for a thunderstorm, and let nature do the work.
Every strike stores **5,000 FE**. Once charged, a glowing energy core spins inside the copper cage.

---

## Copper Cables

- **Bare Wire** — cheap, connects to everything… and **electrocutes** anything that touches it while powered.
- **Insulated Wire** — wrapped in wool, safe, and available in **16 colours**.

Insulated wires only connect to their own colour (or bare wire), so you can run **separate networks side by side** without them merging.

---

## Advanced Furnace

An electric furnace that smelts anything a furnace, blast furnace or smoker can — and **alloys two materials together**.

- **Rose Gold** = Copper + Gold
- **Red Iron Alloy** = Iron + Redstone

Hoppers welcome: inputs from the top and sides, output from the bottom.

---

## And more

- **Redstone Converter** — turns a powered network into a redstone signal.
- **Energy Meter** — look at any FE block, from Electra or any other mod, to read its charge.
- **Rose Gold** and **Red Iron Alloy** blocks, tagged for cross-mod recipes.
